## Laminar Pour Physics

With a two-tier gravity-release system, hydraulic performance happens in two distinct stages. First, aligning the handle magnet lifts a stainless steel ball inside the upper glass brewing chamber, dropping brewed tea cleanly through a fine bottom screen into the 500-milliliter sharing carafe below without splashing or disturbing the settled leaves. Second, pouring from the lower borosilicate vessel delivers an effortless pitcher-like stream: its pinched triangular lip severs surface tension sharply, earning 9.5/10 for splash-free cup filling right at your desk or tasting table without stray rim droplets.

## Strainer & Leaf Expansion Dynamics

Upper chamber diameter governs expansion quality, and Kamjove gives loose-leaf teas generous cylindrical room. Rather than pinning leaves inside a tight metal thimble, water immerses the entire bed evenly across a flat bottom screen. When you trigger the magnetic release valve, water drains vertically downward across the whole leaf mass in roughly four seconds, preventing trapped pockets of stagnant liquor from over-extracting. Fine stainless wire mesh screens out small fragments cleanly, though dusty herbal blends can briefly slow valve drainage.

## Thermal Behavior

Clear double-tier borosilicate glass trades mass thermal retention for crisp visual feedback and instantaneous cooling responsiveness. During 20-second Gongfu flashes with Taiwanese high-mountain oolong or ripe pu-erh, heat remains remarkably steady inside the covered upper steep chamber; once drained into the lower glass server, liquor cools gently toward comfortable sipping temperatures around 65 degrees Celsius. ## Care & Pairing Recommendations

Mechanical clarity depends on keeping the magnetic steel ball and lower drain gasket spotless. After your final infusion, pop out the inner mesh screen, rinse away lodged leaf fibers under warm running water, inspect the silicone collar for stray stems, and soak glass parts periodically in warm baking-soda solution as described in our [Complete Teapot Care Guide](/guides/care-maintenance/complete-teapot-care-guide/).
