## Laminar Pour Physics

With nearly a full liter of water inside thick iron walls, pour control relies heavily on handle leverage and spout geometry. The Old Dutch Sagaform Hobnail places its swing handle pivot slightly forward of center, allowing the wrist to tilt the pot forward smoothly rather than fighting dead weight. Its curved spout yields a sturdy, medium-velocity stream that fills wide mugs quickly without splashing. Because the cast rim is marginally thicker than bespoke Japanese foundry models, slowing down at the final angle can leave a stray droplet at the tip unless you finish with a crisp upward snap.

## Strainer & Leaf Expansion Dynamics

Inside the wide mouth sits a roomy stainless steel mesh basket engineered for family-scale tea batches. At 950 milliliters, the infuser accommodates two to three tablespoons of orthodox Assam, Earl Grey, or herbal spice blends while still leaving room for hydration expansion. Fine mesh sides hold back broken orange pekoe leaf fragments cleanly. Since the basket hangs in the upper two-thirds of the deep chamber, filling the vessel to at least 75 percent capacity ensures complete water immersion across every tea leaf.

## Thermal Behavior

Mass rules thermal physics here. Pre-heated with 150 milliliters of hot tap water before brewing, the Sagaform Hobnail dropped under 4 degrees Celsius over a five-minute black-tea infusion and stayed pleasantly hot in the cup past the half-hour mark—especially when paired with a matching iron trivet. Like most modern decorative cast iron teapots, the interior carries a smooth porcelain-enamel coating designed exclusively for steeping kettle-boiled water rather than open-flame boiling. ## Care & Pairing Recommendations

Long-term care revolves around moisture discipline. Never put enameled cast iron in a dishwasher or scour it with detergent; instead, rinse with warm water, leave the lid off briefly so residual heat evaporates interior dampness, and dry the exposed rim with a soft towel as explained in our [Complete Teapot Care Guide](/guides/care-maintenance/complete-teapot-care-guide/).
