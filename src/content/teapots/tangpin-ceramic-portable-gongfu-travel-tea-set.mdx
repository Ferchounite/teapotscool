## Laminar Pour Physics

Built for carry-on luggage, office desks, and park benches rather than stationary tea tables, the Tangpin 160-milliliter Ceramic Portable Gongfu Set relies on compact proportions and a stubby eagle-beak spout to deliver steady, splash-free pours. In studio flow tests at 90 degrees Celsius, emptying all 160 milliliters into three 45-milliliter tasting cups takes roughly five seconds with clean stream convergence. Scoring 9 out of 10 on laminar cutoff, the glazed lip prevents chin dribbles onto hotel linens or notebook paper, while the rear ceramic loop handle and recessed lid knob give steady one-handed leverage without scorching knuckles during flash steeps.

## Strainer & Leaf Expansion Dynamics

Instead of a tight drop-in metal mesh cylinder that starves rolling leaves in compact pots, the interior wall hides a crescent of seven-to-ten multi-hole ceramic perforations directly behind the spout root. Rolled Taiwanese high-mountain oolongs, Wuyi rock teas, and chunked ripe pu-erh expand cleanly across the full spherical belly of the 160-milliliter chamber. Because water hits every leaf face uniformly during brief 15-to-35-second infusions, flavor saturation stays remarkably vibrant even when brewing from portable insulated water flasks on the road. Just stick to whole-leaf teas rather than shredded dust, which slips past fixed ceramic holes.

## Thermal Behavior

Fired high-density porcelain and glazed stoneware bring dependable mid-range heat conservation despite the pocket-friendly footprint. Once pre-warmed with a quick hot rinse from your travel thermos, the dense 160-milliliter body maintains active 85°C–93°C oolong extraction warmth throughout brief flash pours without cracking under temperature swings. Even better, the vitrified non-porous interior glaze never retains spicy oils—meaning you can switch smoothly from ripe Yunnan pu-erh in the morning to fragrant tieguanyin by afternoon. ## Care & Pairing Recommendations

Packing wet teaware is where sloppy travel sets turn sticky or stale. After finishing your final road session, discard spent leaves, rinse the glazed ceramic pot and tasting cups under warm tap water, wipe completely dry with the included microfiber tea towel before zipping everything inside the foam-molded EVA shell, and empty any caught rinse drops from the compact melamine-and-bamboo tray.
