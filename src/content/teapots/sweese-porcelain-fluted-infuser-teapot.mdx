## Laminar Pour Physics

The Sweese Fluted Porcelain Teapot adopts the timeless rounded English silhouette, pairing a spacious loop handle with a forward-sweeping spout. At 800 milliliters filled, the generous C-handle keeps knuckles safely clear of radiant wall heat while offering crisp two-finger leverage across repeated pours. Its glazed spout discharges a full, brisk stream suited to large breakfast mugs and delicate teacups alike. During testing, flow stayed coherent throughout normal pours, though slowing down at the final two ounces occasionally left one residual droplet beneath the thick porcelain rim unless lifted decisively upright at completion.

## Strainer & Leaf Expansion Dynamics

Hidden beneath the fitted porcelain lid is a cylindrical 18-8 stainless steel infuser basket with clean micro-perforations. Extending roughly two-thirds down the fluted belly, the basket provides generous horizontal spread for orthodox Assam, Darjeeling, and blended Earl Grey leaves while preventing stray petals or bergamot zest from escaping into the pot. When making single-mug quantities under 350 milliliters, tilting water up to the basket floor ensures full saturation; at full 800-milliliter sharing batches, leaves circulate freely with clean extraction.

## Thermal Behavior

High-fired vitrified porcelain outperforms clear glass in maintaining extraction energy. Pre-heated with a brief hot-water swirl, the Sweese 800ml held within 5.5 degrees Celsius across a four-minute steep—ideal for full-bodied black teas requiring sustained heat above 90 degrees Celsius to release malty sweetness without turning thin. Unlike porous unglazed clay, smooth porcelain glaze imparts zero mineral alteration, giving a crisp, transparent cup profile across scented jasmine or spiced chai.
